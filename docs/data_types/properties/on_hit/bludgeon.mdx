# Bludgeon Property

## Description

 The BludgeonProperty introduces additional blunt damage to attacks based on the equipped item.  
This property calculates bludgeoning damage and applies it as bonus damage during melee combat,factoring in the target's **Armor Toughness** to determine the effectiveness.<p>The applied bonus damage is the bludgeon value but capped at the targets Armor Thoughness.

## Data

- **bludgeon**:  A double value indicating the amount of blunt (impact) damage the item can deal.
